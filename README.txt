WAS-D? is an incredibly frustrating platformer where your aim is to reach the hospital (currently not represented in the game. The game level just sort of ends). The frustrating part is that every so often a spin wheel appears and randomises your game controls making them hard to use and remember. The bar in the top left fills up to represent the time you have remaining before they change again.

The time between the wheel appearing can be changed by changing the difficulty in settings (easy = 120, medium = 60 and hard = 30). Or if preferred the wheel can be disabled entirely using the toggle switch in the settings menu. Do note this locks in your current controls as they are even if they have already changed.

Collecting the green RAM around the level adds a bit of time to the interval between wheel spins.

The wooden sign located half way through the level is a checkpoint.

The default (starting) controls are the left and right arrow keys for movement, space to jump and esc to open the menu. The wheel says what your controls will randomise to and if you forget you can check these by pressing esc to open the menu.
